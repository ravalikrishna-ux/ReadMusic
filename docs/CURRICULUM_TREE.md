# ReadMusic — Comprehensive Guitar Course Trees & Curriculum Specification

> **Tuning**: Standard Guitar Tuning (`⑥ E2 – ⑤ A2 – ④ D3 – ③ G3 – ② B3 – ① E4`)
> **Notation Convention**: Standard Guitar Treble Clef (8vb — sounds one octave lower than written: open low E is written `E3`, open high E is written `E5`).
> **Structure**:
> - **Track A: Music Theory for Guitarists** — 8 Modules, 36 Lessons, 40+ iconic 80s/90s/00s Rock & Pop breakdowns + original playable etudes.
> - **Track B: Reading Standard Notation** — 8 Modules, 38 Lessons, Procedural Sight-Reading Generators, culminating in 4 Full Multi-Section Capstone Sheet Music Songs.
> - **Track C: Integrated Path (Both)** — 16 synchronized milestones interleaving Theory & Reading so each track reinforces the other.
> - **Free Tier**: Module T1 (Theory) and Module R1 (Reading) are **100% unlocked** free. Modules 2–8 unlock via the **$4.99 one-time purchase**.
